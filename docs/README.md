# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, lifecycle-based approach to project management designed to keep work aligned, measurable, and transparent across cross-functional teams. The process begins with project initiation to validate the business need, define success metrics, and align stakeholders before committing to delivery. Once approved, teams move into planning to break work into shippable increments, document acceptance criteria, manage dependencies, and create milestones and release expectations. The execution phase focuses on daily coordination, issue tracking, and continuous quality review to keep delivery on pace without losing focus on customer value.

At the center of this model are clear role definitions and communication rhythms. Product managers define outcomes and priorities, project managers coordinate delivery, dependencies, and stakeholder updates, and developers build and validate work through tests, reviews, and iterative improvements. Risk management is treated as an ongoing discipline, with a risk register, escalation paths, and regular review cycles used to identify and mitigate threats before they become blockers. The team also places strong emphasis on quality assurance, with CI checks, security scanning, test coverage, and smoke testing required before release. After each milestone or release, the team closes the loop with retrospectives to capture lessons learned, assign action items, and improve the process over time.
